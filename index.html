How to read this map

History is not a single clean polygon.

Worldline Atlas separates direct evidence from scholarly reconstruction and speculation. Every feature can carry a date range, confidence score, evidence class, source list, and notes about disagreement.

This first release is a product and data-model prototype. Its sample geometries are deliberately simplified and are not intended as publication-grade historical boundaries.
