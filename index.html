How to read the atlas

The land image is modern. The settlement layer changes through time.

The satellite surface is a 2024 Sentinel-2 cloudless mosaic. It provides recognizable terrain, not a reconstruction of historical vegetation, coastlines, rivers, or buildings.

Historical settlement records come from OpenHistoricalMap vector tiles, a live Wikidata query for the visible map area, and a small curated set of early archaeological sites. Features are shown only when their available dates overlap the selected year.

Coverage is uneven. A blank area means no compatible record was returned, not that nobody lived there. Dates can represent occupation phases, founding traditions, administrative status, or archaeological estimates depending on the source.
