How to read the atlas

Worldline separates observed imagery from historical reconstruction.

Modern mode uses a Sentinel-2 cloudless mosaic. Reconstructed mode progressively removes modern surface detail as you zoom in and replaces available structures with date-filtered OpenHistoricalMap footprints.

Historical footprints are extruded using recorded height or level information when present. Missing heights use a clearly identified estimated fallback. This first reconstruction surface is therefore a dated vector model, not a photograph of the past.

The long-term pipeline will add reviewed historical land-cover rasters and locally rendered orthophoto tiles built from cited geometry, materials, vegetation, and competing scholarly interpretations.

Coverage is uneven. A blank area means no compatible record was returned, not that nobody lived there.
