How to read the atlas

The selected time determines the Earth you see.

Earth History is the default. From the present to 1.8 billion years ago, coastlines come from the pinned CAO2024 GPlates reconstruction model. Simplified keyframes are stored directly in this GitHub repository so common ages render immediately; the live model is a background refinement and fallback.

Before 1.8 billion years ago, exact global coastlines are not scientifically defensible. Worldline therefore switches to a clearly labelled schematic planet rather than inventing continental positions.

Human History is a separate, denser timeline beginning 300,000 years ago. It adds archaeological sites, historical settlements, dated map records, and contextual summaries without pretending that sparse evidence represents complete coverage.

Green and orange life markers are Paleobiology Database fossil records placed at published or model-reconstructed paleocoordinates. They are evidence locations, not complete organism ranges. A blank region does not mean life was absent there.
